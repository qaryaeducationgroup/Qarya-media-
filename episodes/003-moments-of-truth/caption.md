One airline boss counted 50 million moments a year that decided whether his company would succeed or fail.

In the 1980s, Jan Carlzon ran Scandinavian Airlines (SAS). He called every contact with a customer a "moment of truth": about 10 million customers a year, each meeting about 5 employees for about 15 seconds. In hospitality, those moments are the greeting at the door, the call to housekeeping and the goodbye at check-out.

Sources: Jan Carlzon, Moments of Truth (1987), as summarised by Wikipedia and Forbes (2024).

Most hotels won't let you into your room until 3 or 4 PM. There's a reason.

Check-out is usually around 11 AM, and check-in comes hours later (times vary by hotel). That gap is housekeeping time: the room is vacated, stripped, cleaned and inspected before the next guest arrives. As hospitality consultant Gary Vallen put it: "It's a function of housekeeping."

Sources: The Washington Post (via Hospitality Net); InsideHook.

Walk into a really good hotel and you notice the smell first. That's by design.

Many hotels create a signature scent and diffuse it through the lobby. Smell is the one sense that skips the brain's relay station, the thalamus, and goes straight to the areas that handle emotion and memory. So months later, one breath can bring the whole stay back.

Great hospitality doesn't just design rooms. It designs memories.

Sources: Harvard Gazette (2020); Harvard Medical School magazine (2024).

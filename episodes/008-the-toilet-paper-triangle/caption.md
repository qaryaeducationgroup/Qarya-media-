That little triangle on hotel toilet paper is a message.

In 1989, writer David Feldman asked the biggest hotel chains why they do it, and got the same answer: the fold shows the bathroom has been cleaned and nobody has used the roll since. Who started it? Nobody has documented that.

Sources: Wikipedia, "Hotel toilet paper folding" (citing Feldman, 1989); Mental Floss.

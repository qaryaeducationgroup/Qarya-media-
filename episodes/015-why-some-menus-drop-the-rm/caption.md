Some menus leave out the currency sign. There's a study behind it.

Cornell researchers gave 201 tables at one restaurant menus with prices written as words, with a dollar sign, or as plain numbers. Tables with plain numbers spent about 8% more. It was one restaurant, and the researchers said it raises more questions than it answers.

Sources: Cornell Chronicle (2009); Yang, Kimes & Sessarego, Int. J. of Hospitality Management (2009).

Most people will choose more pain, if it ends better.

In a 1993 study, volunteers held a hand in cold water twice: once for 60 seconds, and once for 90 seconds where the last 30 were slightly less cold. 22 of 32 chose to repeat the longer one. Our memory leans on the peak and the end, not the length. Psychologists call it the peak-end rule.

In hospitality, that's why the goodbye deserves as much care as the welcome.

Sources: Kahneman et al., Psychological Science (1993); Redelmeier et al., Pain (2003).

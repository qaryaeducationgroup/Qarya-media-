Why are hotel beds almost always white?

In 1999 Westin introduced its all-white Heavenly Bed, and other chains answered by upgrading their own beds. Westin didn't invent white sheets, but it's credited with popularising the look. White tells guests at a glance that the bed is clean, and it can be bleached.
